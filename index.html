Chun Lin Photography

I started doing photography since 2020 after I bought my first camera, the Ricoh GRiii. Soon after, I got the Fujifilm XT4 and then the Fujifilm GFX 50Sii in late 2025. Most of the pictures you will find are my personal photos from vacations, trips, outings, etc. A few were paid shoots. I regret not documenting my life more, not going out enough, and not being creative enough when I was young. Photography has been a great push for me to do more and romanticize my life as well as the lives of my family and friends and hopefully, you as well. I would love to meet other photographers so we can go on photography-focused trips/walks/events together. I would also love to meet people who want to model and work with me as well as anyone who wants a paid session for whatever focus they desire. My editing style depends on what I am shooting for. A large part of my works is to document my life, so the editing leans on how I remember the scene plus light changes (no photoshop/AI/removing/adding items). I hope you enjoy my work and please, feel free to reach out!
